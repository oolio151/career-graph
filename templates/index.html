ABOUT THIS WORKSPACE

A first look at your next chapter.

Career Graph connects the HackUMBC DoIT dataset to observed career pathways, course suggestions, alumni activities, and an advisor that summarizes dataset evidence.

The supplied track dataset is synthetic and does not describe real UMBC students or outcomes. Salary summaries use nominal dollars for the selected job start year. Course coverage reflects exposure, not mastery or hiring probability.

The advisor uses calculated dataset summaries rather than a live generative AI model. Bookmarks and activity interests stay in your browser.
