ABOUT THIS WORKSPACE

A first look at your next chapter.

Career Graph is a frontend prototype for the HackUMBC 2026 DoIT track. Explore example career connections, save roles, and try a scripted advisor conversation.

Roles, salary ranges, skills, and activity matches here are hand-authored examples, not findings from the track dataset. The supplied track dataset is itself synthetic and does not describe real UMBC students or outcomes.

The live AI advisor and dataset-backed matching are future integrations.
