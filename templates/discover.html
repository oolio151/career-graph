    How these numbers work

    All records and employers are synthetic. The snapshot is September 15, 2026. These are historical patterns, not open jobs, placement odds, or evidence that an activity causes an outcome.

    The group is bachelor’s graduates in your major. Master’s records are left out, because the file keeps only each person’s highest degree. “No response” stays in the graduate count as unknown, and is left out of the first-job shares.

    Each graduate counts once. “What came next” uses only the second job, and only people who have one recorded. Recent graduates have had less time to move. Matching your current GPA or internship count to a graduate’s final record is an exploration, not a recommendation. Small groups can make a percentage swing on a handful of people.

    Course skills are subjects covered by courses you passed, not a verified skill check. Your resume is stored on this computer and is not used in these results.
